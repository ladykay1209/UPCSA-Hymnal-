Privacy Policy

UPCSA Hymnal Mobile Application

Last updated: 24 November 2026

The United Presbyterian Church in Southern Africa respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information of individuals who use the UPCSA Hymnal mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored and protected when you use the application.

1. About the Application

UPCSA Hymnal is a mobile application developed to support worship, fellowship, spiritual growth and the preservation of Christian hymnody within the United Presbyterian Church in Southern Africa.

The application provides access to hymns, devotional materials, fellowship resources and other church-related content intended to support the spiritual life of its users.

2. Information We Collect

The information processed by the application depends on the features you choose to use.

This may include:

  • Account information, where account registration or sign-in is provided.
  • Information voluntarily submitted by users through application features.
  • Technical information necessary for application functionality.
  • Diagnostic and crash information, where enabled through the services used by the application.

We aim to limit the collection of personal information to what is necessary for providing and maintaining the application's services.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed through the application may be used for the following purposes:

  • Providing and maintaining application features.
  • Supporting account registration and authentication.
  • Delivering hymns, devotional and fellowship resources.
  • Improving application performance and reliability.
  • Identifying and resolving technical problems.
  • Maintaining and updating application content.

4. Third-Party Services

Certain application features may rely on third-party service providers to support authentication, cloud infrastructure, content delivery and application functionality.

These providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms of service.

Where applicable, you may review Google's privacy policy:

Google Privacy Policy

5. Device Permissions

Depending on the features you use, UPCSA Hymnal may request access to certain device functions, including:

  • Internet access to retrieve application content and services.
  • Photo-library or storage access when saving or selecting images.
  • Notification permissions where notifications are enabled.

You can manage available device permissions through your device settings.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be processed by service providers where necessary to operate, maintain and improve application functionality.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or lawful authority.

7. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are taken to protect information processed through the application against unauthorised access, alteration, loss or disclosure.

Information is retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

UPCSA Hymnal provides church and fellowship resources that may be used by individuals of different ages.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children beyond what is necessary for the application's legitimate functionality.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable privacy legislation, users may have the right to:

  • Request access to their personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information.
  • Raise concerns about how their information is processed.

Privacy-related enquiries and requests may be submitted using the contact details provided below.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the application, its services or applicable legislation.

The latest version will always be made available through this webpage.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests relating to this Privacy Policy or the handling of personal information through UPCSA Hymnal, please contact the application team.
